About Department

Vision

To provide quality education in computer applications and help students develop strong technical and professional skills.

Mission

To impart quality technical education through balanced theoretical and practical learning, preparing students to solve complex industry problems.

Objectives

  • Provide strong algorithmic, software design, and programming foundations.
  • Promote industry interaction through workshops, internships, and live projects.
  • Encourage multidisciplinary research in artificial intelligence, cloud, and data science.
